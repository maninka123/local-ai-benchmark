# Case 01 — investigation report

## 1. Executive summary

The prototype was present on the lab bench at 18:00 and absent at 18:18. A teal-coated figure appears in the east corridor at 18:10 and with a visually matching triangle-marked case at the east exit at 18:14, matching Nora’s credential events. The most economical reconstruction is that Nora, or someone using her coat and credential, acquired the prototype during the interval and took it toward/out through the east exit. Nora is the probable responsible individual for that movement, but identity, the pickup itself, object uniqueness, and authority to move it are not established. [V01, V02, V03, V04, V05, V09, D01, D03]

**No responsible individual is definitively identified.**

### Plausible alternatives

- Someone else may have used Nora’s assigned coat and credential; D03 explicitly prevents biometric attribution. [V03, V04, D01, D03]
- The exit case could be a look-alike, or the prototype could have been moved with permission. Neither alternative is demonstrated, but the exhibits do not rule them out. [V04, V09, D02, D03]
- A different person could have used the service blind spot and removed or relocated the prototype independently; this fits a coverage gap but has less positive support than the teal-figure sequence. [V01, V10, D03]

## 8. Confidence and limitations

- **Overall:** Moderate for Nora as the person who moved the prototype; not definitive.
- **Observed change:** High: the reference object is present at 18:00 and absent from the bench at 18:18.
- **Route and possession:** Moderate to high for the teal figure carrying a matching case at the east exit; moderate for it being the same prototype.
- **Individual identity:** Moderate: coat and credential align twice, but neither authenticates identity.
- **Intent or authorisation:** Undetermined: no permission, ownership, or intent evidence establishes misconduct.

- Synthetic schematics simplify persons, objects, and locations. They support only visible features, not faces, fingerprints, serial numbers, precise distances, or hidden contents. [V01, V02, V03, V04, V05, V06, V07, V08, V09]
- Badge credentials and coat assignments are identity proxies; sharing, misuse, or reassignment is not ruled out or positively demonstrated. [D01, D03]
- No uninterrupted removal footage, complete access history, authorisation record, recovery evidence, or full-window alibi is supplied. [V01, V02, V05, V10, D01, D02]
- Confidence labels are qualitative judgments, not calibrated probabilities or findings of legal guilt. [V02, V04, V05, D01, D03]

Case 03 — investigation report

1. Executive summary

The prototype is present at 18:00 and absent at 18:18. At 18:14, credentials for Eli and Maya are recorded entering the service door while a frame shows two indistinguishable gray figures with no visible prototype. This creates investigative leads for both but does not identify either figure, show object custody, or connect a person to removal. The defensible reconstruction ends with an unobserved movement of the prototype sometime after 18:00 and by 18:18; neither Eli, Maya, nor another person can be conclusively selected.

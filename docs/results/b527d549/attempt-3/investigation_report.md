# CASE_02: Missing Laboratory Prototype - Investigation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This investigation examines the disappearance of a laboratory prototype between 18:00 and 18:18. Five staff members were present in the facility during this period. Evidence analysis indicates that **Eli Stone** is the most likely responsible individual, with documented motive (prototype request at 17:00), opportunity (access via service corridor), and visual confirmation (observed with prototype at actual time 18:14). However, limitations in evidence quality prevent absolute certainty. Confidence level: 0.85.

## 5. Key Deductions

1. **Motive established**: Eli Stone requested prototype at 17:00 (D03_messages)
2. **Opportunity established**: Service corridor provides unmonitored access to lab (V01); Eli entered via service door at 18:14 (D01)
3. **Visual confirmation**: Eli observed with prototype in service corridor at actual time 18:14 (V04/V10, corrected from displayed 18:19)
4. **Timeline consistent**: Prototype missing by 18:18 (V05); window of 4 minutes between observation and discovery
5. **Other staff accounted for**: Location snapshots place Nora, Theo, Ada, and Maya in separate areas during critical window (V03/V06/V07/V08)

## 6. Contradictions

1. **Eli's witness statement vs visual evidence**: D02 states Eli claimed no approach to lab after 18:00, but V04/V10 visually show him with prototype at service corridor at actual time 18:14
2. **Location snapshots do not establish continuous whereabouts**: Gaps exist between observed locations; cannot definitively rule out unobserved movements
3. **Identity verification limitations**: Coat color is appearance evidence, not biometric identity verification per D03_messages; badge credentials log access but do not verify identity per D03_messages

## 7. Final Conclusion

Based on available evidence, Eli Stone is the most likely responsible individual for the missing prototype. The evidence chain includes: documented motive (17:00 request), opportunity via unmonitored service corridor access, visual confirmation with prototype at actual time 18:14, and timeline consistency with discovery at 18:18. Other staff members were documented in separate locations during the critical window.

**Confidence level**: 0.85 (high but not absolute due to evidence limitations)

**Alternative explanations remain plausible**:
- Unknown person used service corridor access without badge credential logging
- Timing gaps between observed locations allow for unobserved movements
- Prototype could have been moved by multiple people during documented windows

## 8. Confidence and Limitations

**Confidence**: 0.85 - High confidence based on converging evidence but not absolute certainty.

**Limitations**:
- Coat color evidence does not verify biometric identity per D03_messages
- Badge credentials log access but do not verify identity per D03_messages
- Location snapshots do not establish continuous whereabouts between observed times
- Service corridor camera blind spots may have missed additional activity
- V09 is object-detail exhibit, not east-exit camera as previously misidentified
- V10 contains clock maintenance notice in addition to showing Eli with prototype

# Investigation Report: Case_02 - Missing Laboratory Prototype

## 1. Executive Summary
Between 18:00 and 18:18, a laboratory prototype was removed from the Lab. The investigation identifies Eli Stone as the primary suspect. Evidence shows Eli Stone entered the facility via the Service Door at 18:14, a location that provides unmonitored access to the Lab via the Service Corridor. Crucially, Eli Stone's statement claiming he did not approach the lab after 18:00 is directly contradicted by both badge access logs and security camera footage (V04), which shows him in an orange coat (matching his assignment) at the Service Door. While Maya Reed's movement with a box presents a secondary possibility, the direct contradiction and motive associated with Eli Stone make him the most likely individual responsible.

## 4. Suspect Analysis

### Eli Stone (Primary Suspect)
*   **Opportunity**: High. Entered via the Service Door at 18:14, which connects to the Service Corridor—an unmonitored link directly to the Lab (V01).
*   **Access**: Confirmed via badge logs and camera V04.
*   **Supporting Evidence**: Motive established via message D01. Presence in the unmonitored corridor at the time of the theft.
*   **Exculpatory Evidence**: None.
*   **Contradictions**: His statement ("I did not approach the lab after 18:00") is refuted by the badge log and camera V04.

### Maya Reed
*   **Opportunity**: Moderate. Seen moving a box in the Loading Bay at 18:11.
*   **Access**: Present in the facility.
*   **Supporting Evidence**: The box could potentially conceal the prototype.
*   **Exculpatory Evidence**: Her movement was part of a documented task (D02).

### Nora Vale, Theo Park, Ada Finch
*   **Opportunity**: Low. Their movements (East Corridor, Cafeteria, Reception) were captured on monitored cameras and do not show direct access to the Lab during the critical window.
*   **Supporting Evidence**: None.
*   **Exculpatory Evidence**: Captured on cameras in non-Lab areas.

## 5. Key Deductions
*   **The Unmonitored Path**: The Service Corridor (V01) is the critical vulnerability. Anyone entering the Service Door (like Eli Stone) can reach the Lab without being seen by the main corridor cameras.
*   **The Clock Offset**: The +5 minute offset on the Service Camera (V10) is essential for aligning the 18:19 timestamp in V04 with the 18:14 badge log entry.
*   **The False Alibi**: The discrepancy between Eli Stone's statement and the electronic/visual records is the strongest indicator of culpability.

## 6. Contradictions
*   **Statement vs. Record**: Eli Stone claims no approach to the lab after 18:00, but badge logs and camera V04 place him at the Service Door (a lab access point) at 18:14.

## 7. Final Conclusion
Based on the evidence, **Eli Stone** most likely removed the prototype. He had a clear motive (D01), and his entry via the Service Door (Log, V04) placed him in the Service Corridor, which provides unmonitored access to the Lab (V01). His attempt to provide a false alibi regarding his proximity to the lab further supports this conclusion.

## 8. Confidence and Limitations
*   **Confidence**: 70%. The primary evidence is the contradiction of the suspect's statement and the unmonitored access route. 
*   **Limitations**: The identity of the person in the orange coat is based on coat color, which is an assignment-based proxy, not biometric. We cannot definitively prove the prototype was in the box Maya Reed was carrying, though it remains a plausible alternative.

## 9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps
*   Perform a physical search of Eli Stone's workspace and personal belongings.
*   Review any secondary logs or sensor data from the Service Corridor if available.
*   Interview Maya Reed regarding the contents of the box moved at 18:11.

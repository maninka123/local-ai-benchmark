Investigation Report: Case 03 - Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

On 18:18, a laboratory prototype was discovered missing from the LAB. The theft occurred between 18:00 (last confirmed presence) and 18:18. Evidence suggests the perpetrator likely utilized the unmonitored service corridor to bypass primary security cameras. While no direct visual of the theft exists, the timing of personnel movements and the presence of a transportable container (cardboard box) point toward a coordinated or opportunistic removal during the 18:10-18:14 window.

2. Evidence Inventory

  • Visual Exhibits (V01-V10): Includes floor plans (V01), camera snapshots (V02-V08), object details (V09), and security blind spot documentation (V10).
  • Access Logs (D01-D05): Badge entry records for Nora Vale, Theo Park, Ada Finch, Eli Stone, and Maya Reed.
  • Communications: Internal messages regarding prototype necessity (Eli) and box movement (Maya).
  • Witness Statements: Unverified accounts from staff members regarding their locations.

3. Timeline

  • 17:00: Eli Stone expresses interest in the prototype via message.
  • 17:10: Maya Reed instructed to move cardboard consumables box to loading bay.
  • 17:20: Security note confirms service corridor is a camera blind spot.
  • 18:00: Prototype confirmed present in LAB (V02).
  • 18:10: Nora Vale enters East Corridor (V03).
  • 18:11: Maya Reed seen in Loading Bay with box (V08).
  • 18:12: Theo Park enters Cafeteria (V06).
  • 18:13: Ada Finch enters Reception (V07).
  • 18:14: Eli Stone and Maya Reed enter via Service Door (V04).
  • 18:18: Prototype confirmed missing from LAB (V05).

4. Suspect Analysis

  • Maya Reed: High suspicion. Was seen with a box (V08) at 18:11 and entered the service area at 18:14 (D05). The box provides a viable concealment method.
  • Eli Stone: High suspicion. Expressed motive at 17:00. Entered the service area at 18:14 (D04) alongside Maya.
  • Nora Vale: Low suspicion. Movement in East Corridor (V03) is consistent with her statement and does not place her in the service blind spot.
  • Theo Park / Ada Finch: Low suspicion. Movements (V06, V07) are consistent with their roles and do not intersect with the LAB/Service Corridor during the critical window.

5. Key Deductions

  • The Service Corridor (V01) is the critical vulnerability due to its unmonitored status (V10).
  • The cardboard box (V08) is the most likely vessel for transporting the prototype without detection.
  • The 18:14 entry of both Eli and Maya through the service door suggests they were active in the area where the blind spot exists.

6. Contradictions

  • Coat Color Discrepancy: The SERVICE DOOR camera (V04) shows individuals in grey coats, whereas badge logs (D04, D05) and staff assignments suggest Eli (orange) and Maya (purple) should be wearing different colors. This may indicate a change in attire or a limitation in camera color rendering.
  • Witness vs. Log: While Maya's statement about the box is supported by V08, the timing of her entry via the service door (18:14) occurs after she was seen in the loading bay (18:11), implying movement through the facility that is not fully captured by cameras.

7. Final Conclusion

No single individual can be definitively identified as the thief due to the lack of coverage in the service corridor. However, the convergence of motive (Eli), means (Maya's box), and opportunity (both entering the service area during the window of disappearance) makes Maya Reed and Eli Stone the primary persons of interest. The theft most likely occurred via the unmonitored link between the LAB and the Service Corridor.

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: 60%. The evidence strongly points to the service corridor as the route, but the lack of visual evidence in that area prevents a higher confidence level.
  • Limitations: The service corridor is a known blind spot (V10). Camera color accuracy is uncertain (V04). Badge logs confirm entry but not the contents of any carried items.

9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps

  • Review physical contents of the cardboard box mentioned in the 17:10 message.
  • Inspect the loading bay and service area for any discarded packaging or traces of the prototype.
  • Conduct formal interviews with Maya Reed and Eli Stone regarding their movements between 18:11 and 18:14.

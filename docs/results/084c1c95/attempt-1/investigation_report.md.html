Investigation Report: Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

On [Date], a laboratory prototype was reported missing between 18:00 and 18:18. Based on access logs, witness statements, and facility schematics, the most likely scenario involves the use of the unmonitored service corridor. Maya Reed, carrying a cardboard box, and Eli Stone both entered this corridor at 18:14. Maya Reed is identified as the primary suspect due to the availability of a concealment method.

2. Evidence Inventory

  • V01: Facility schematic showing the unmonitored service corridor link to the LAB.
  • V02: Camera footage at 18:00 confirming the prototype's presence.
  • D01: Access logs showing entries to the EAST_CORRIDOR, CAFETERIA, RECEPTION, and SERVICE_DOOR.
  • D02: Witness statement regarding Maya Reed carrying a cardboard box at 18:11.
  • D03: Internal messages indicating Eli Stone's motive and the existence of a camera blind spot in the service corridor.

3. Timeline

  • 17:00: Eli Stone expresses interest in the prototype (D03).
  • 17:20: Security confirms the service corridor is a camera blind spot (D03).
  • 18:00: Prototype confirmed present in LAB (V02).
  • 18:11: Maya Reed observed with a cardboard box (D02).
  • 18:14: Eli Stone and Maya Reed enter the unmonitored SERVICE_DOOR (D01).
  • 18:18: Prototype discovered missing.

4. Suspect Analysis

  • Maya Reed: High Suspicion. Entered the unmonitored corridor at 18:14. Possessed a cardboard box at 18:11, providing an ideal way to hide the prototype.
  • Eli Stone: Medium Suspicion. Entered the unmonitored corridor at 18:14. Had expressed a specific need for the prototype earlier that day (D03).
  • Nora Vale, Theo Park, Ada Finch: Low Suspicion. Their movements (entering East Corridor, Cafeteria, and Reception respectively) do not place them in the unmonitored area or provide concealment methods.

5. Key Deductions

  • The theft occurred after 18:00 (V02) and before 18:18.
  • The service corridor (V01) was the likely escape route as it is a known camera blind spot (D03).
  • The presence of a cardboard box (D02) is the critical factor in enabling the removal of the item without detection.

6. Contradictions

  • Eli Stone claims he did not approach the lab, yet access logs (D01) place him entering the service door, which leads directly to the lab.

7. Final Conclusion

It is highly probable that the prototype was removed via the service corridor at approximately 18:14. Maya Reed's possession of a cardboard box at the time of entry into the blind spot makes her the most likely individual to have successfully transported the item out of the lab.

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: 75%
  • Limitations: No direct visual evidence of the theft exists due to the camera blind spot. The conclusion relies on circumstantial evidence (timing and concealment method).

9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps

  • Inspect the contents of the cardboard box mentioned by Maya Reed.
  • Review footage from the loading bay/exit areas where the box was intended to be moved.
  • Interview Eli Stone regarding his presence at the service door.

Investigation Report: Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

The laboratory prototype was stolen between 18:00 and 18:18. Evidence suggests that Maya Reed entered the Lab via the Service Corridor (a known camera blind spot) at approximately 18:14. She was observed inside the Lab with a cardboard box. Eli Stone, who had a stated motive for the prototype earlier in the day, was also present in the Service Corridor during the incident, suggesting potential collusion.

2. Evidence Inventory

  • V01: Synthetic Schematic (identifies Service Corridor as a blind spot)
  • V02: Lab Camera 18:00 (confirms prototype presence)
  • V03-V05: Locations of Nora, Theo, and Ada (exculpatory)
  • V06-V09: Movements of Eli and Maya in the Service Corridor and Lab
  • V10: Lab Camera (confirms prototype missing)
  • D01: Access Logs (entry times)
  • D02: Witness Statements
  • D03: Internal Messages (motive and logistics)

4. Suspect Analysis

  • Maya Reed: High suspicion. Seen in the Lab with a box (V07) after entering the Service Door (D01).
  • Eli Stone: High suspicion. Had motive (D03) and was in the Service Corridor with Maya (V06, V09).
  • Nora Vale: Low suspicion. Observed in East Corridor (V03, V08).
  • Theo Park: Low suspicion. Observed in Cafeteria (V04).
  • Ada Finch: Low suspicion. Observed at Reception (V05).

5. Key Deductions

  • The Service Corridor provided a covert route to the Lab (V01, D03).
  • Maya Reed's presence in the Lab with a box (V07) directly correlates with the disappearance of the prototype.
  • Eli Stone's motive (D03) and presence in the blind spot (V06, V09) suggest he may have coordinated the theft.

6. Contradictions

  • Eli Stone claims he did not approach the lab (D02), but visual evidence (V06, V09) places him in the Service Corridor, which is a direct link to the Lab.

7. Final Conclusion

Maya Reed is the primary suspect for the theft of the prototype. She utilized the unmonitored Service Corridor to enter the Lab and was seen carrying a box, likely containing the prototype. Eli Stone is a likely accomplice given his motive and proximity.

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: 0.9
  • Limitations: No direct footage of the prototype being placed in the box.

9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps

  • Search Maya Reed's belongings and the "cardboard supply box" mentioned in D03.
  • Interview Eli Stone regarding his presence in the Service Corridor.
  • Check for any other unmonitored links to the Lab.

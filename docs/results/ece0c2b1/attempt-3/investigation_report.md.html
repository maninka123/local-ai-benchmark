Investigation Report: CASE_01 — Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

A prototype (grey box with black triangular marking) was last confirmed present in the LAB at 18:00 (V02) and discovered missing at 18:18 (V05). A person wearing a teal work coat was observed carrying the prototype at the EAST EXIT at 18:14 (V04), and badge credential "Nora Vale" was used at EAST_CORRIDOR (18:10) and EAST_EXIT (18:14) (D01). However, per security notice D03, doors log badge credentials—not biometric identity—and coat color indicates appearance, not identity. The SERVICE CORRIDOR provides an unmonitored route from LAB to EAST CORRIDOR (V01, V10), creating alternative explanations for how the prototype could have been removed. No responsible individual can be identified with confidence from available evidence. Confidence: 35%.

5. Key Deductions

  1. Prototype was removed between 18:00 and 18:14. V02 confirms presence at 18:00; V04 shows it outside LAB at 18:14.

  2. The SERVICE CORRIDOR provides an unmonitored route from LAB to EAST CORRIDOR (V01, V10). This creates a plausible path for prototype removal without camera coverage.

  3. Badge credentials do not prove identity. Per D03, doors log badge credentials—not biometric identity. The person using "Nora Vale" credential may not be Nora Vale.

  4. Coat color indicates appearance, not identity. Per D03, coat descriptions are from shift assignments. A teal coat does not confirm the wearer is Nora Vale.

  5. Brief location snapshots cannot account for continuous whereabouts. Camera images represent single moments; persons could have moved between snapshots.

  6. No evidence directly proves who removed the prototype or their intent. Motive (Eli's project need) alone is insufficient proof of responsibility.

7. Final Conclusion

What most likely happened: A person wearing a teal work coat was observed carrying the prototype at the EAST EXIT at 18:14 (V04). Badge credential "Nora Vale" was used at EAST_CORRIDOR (18:10) and EAST_EXIT (18:14) (D01). The prototype was removed from the LAB between 18:00 and 18:14, likely through the unmonitored SERVICE CORRIDOR (V01, V10).

Observations supporting this conclusion:

  • V02 confirms prototype present at 18:00; V05 confirms absence at 18:18
  • V04 shows person in teal coat with prototype at EAST EXIT at 18:14
  • D01 records "Nora Vale" credential at EAST_CORRIDOR and EAST_EXIT
  • V01 and V10 confirm unmonitored SERVICE CORRIDOR route

Plausible alternative explanations:

  1. Someone other than Nora Vale used her badge credential to enter EAST_CORRIDOR and EAST_EXIT
  2. The prototype was moved through the unmonitored SERVICE CORRIDOR by an unidentified person
  3. The teal-coated individual at EAST EXIT may not be the same person who removed the prototype from LAB
  4. Eli Stone (who has motive per D03) could have used the service corridor without being visually recorded

Can a responsible individual be identified? No. The available evidence does not support identifying a specific responsible individual with confidence. Badge credentials and coat colors cannot establish biometric identity (D03). The unmonitored SERVICE CORRIDOR creates multiple plausible paths for prototype removal by unidentified persons.

# Investigation Report: Missing Laboratory Prototype

## 1. Executive Summary
The laboratory prototype was stolen between 18:00 and 18:14 on the date of the incident. Direct visual evidence confirms that **Nora Vale** (identified by her teal work coat) exited the facility carrying the prototype. While other staff members were present in various locations, their movements do not correlate with the theft. Nora's witness statement claiming she "only visited the east corridor and left" is a misleading partial truth designed to obscure her actions.

## 4. Suspect Analysis

*   **Nora Vale (Teal Coat)**:
    *   **Opportunity**: Present in the EAST CORRIDOR adjacent to the LAB at 18:10.
    *   **Evidence**: Seen exiting the facility with the prototype at 18:14 (V04).
    *   **Contradiction**: Her statement that she "only visited the east corridor and left" is technically true but omits the theft, making it a deceptive alibi.
    *   **Conclusion**: Primary suspect. Directly observed taking the item.

*   **Eli Stone (Orange Coat)**:
    *   **Motive**: Needed the prototype for his project (D03).
    *   **Evidence**: No visual or access log evidence placing him near the LAB during the theft window.
    *   **Conclusion**: Unlikely based on available evidence.

*   **Maya Reed (Purple Coat)**:
    *   **Opportunity**: Seen at LOADING BAY at 18:11 with a cardboard box (V08).
    *   **Evidence**: Her statement about moving consumables is corroborated by the visual of her carrying a box.
    *   **Conclusion**: Alibi supported by evidence.

*   **Theo Park (Blue Coat)**:
    *   **Opportunity**: Seen in CAFETERIA at 18:12 (V06).
    *   **Evidence**: Access log and camera footage corroborate his presence in the cafeteria.
    *   **Conclusion**: Alibi supported by evidence.

*   **Ada Finch (Red Coat)**:
    *   **Opportunity**: Seen at RECEPTION at 18:13 (V07).
    *   **Evidence**: Access log and camera footage corroborate her presence at reception.
    *   **Conclusion**: Alibi supported by evidence.

## 5. Key Deductions
1.  **Direct Observation**: V04 provides direct visual proof of Nora Vale carrying the prototype out of the building.
2.  **Path Analysis**: The EAST CORRIDOR (V03) is adjacent to the LAB, providing access. The SERVICE CORRIDOR (V10) is a blind spot, but Nora's presence in the East Corridor makes it unnecessary to assume she used the service route.
3.  **Deception**: Nora's statement is a classic example of omission, allowing her to claim innocence while technically telling the truth about her location.

## 6. Contradictions
*   **Nora's Statement vs. Visual Evidence**: Nora claims she "only visited the east corridor and left." While she did visit the corridor, she also stole the prototype, which is a significant omission of fact regarding the incident.

## 7. Final Conclusion
**Nora Vale** is responsible for the theft of the laboratory prototype. She was observed in the adjacent East Corridor at 18:10 and exiting the facility with the prototype at 18:14. Her witness statement is misleading. The other suspects have corroborated alibis or lack of opportunity.

## 8. Confidence and Limitations
*   **Confidence**: High (95%). Direct visual evidence (V04) confirms the theft and identifies the perpetrator.
*   **Limitations**: The Service Corridor is a blind spot, so we cannot definitively rule out that someone else moved the prototype from the Lab to the East Corridor before Nora took it. However, Nora is the only one observed with the item exiting the facility.

## 9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps
1.  **Interview Nora Vale**: Confront her with V04 and ask for an explanation of how she acquired the prototype in the East Corridor.
2.  **Check Nora's Belongings**: Verify if the prototype is currently in her possession or vehicle.
3.  **Review Access Logs for Service Corridor**: Although blind, check if any badge swipes occurred there to rule out an accomplice.

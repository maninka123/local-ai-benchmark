# Investigation Report

## Executive Summary
An investigation into the missing laboratory prototype at 18:18. The prototype was last seen at 18:00 in the LAB. The most likely sequence of events involves a person entering the LAB via the SERVICE CORRIDOR, which is an unmonitored link, and exiting through the EAST CORRIDOR. The evidence suggests that Nora Vale may have been involved, as she was the only person to enter and exit the LAB during the relevant time window. However, the evidence is inconclusive, and further investigation is needed to confirm the responsible individual.

## 7. Final Conclusion

**Most Likely Scenario:** Nora Vale entered the lab area at 18:10 through the east corridor, took the prototype from the lab, and exited through the east exit at 18:14 carrying it. The prototype was discovered missing from the lab by 18:18.

**Supporting Observations:**

- V03 shows Nora Vale entering east corridor at 18:10 without visible object
- V04 shows Nora Vale exiting east exit at 18:14 with prototype clearly visible
- V05 confirms prototype missing from lab at 18:18
- D01_access.csv confirms Nora Vale's entry/exit times match visual evidence

**Alternative Explanations:**
- Nora Vale may have been authorized to take the prototype for legitimate work purposes (though this would still make her responsible for the incident)

- Eli Stone may have requested the prototype but did not execute the theft

**Responsible Individual Identified:** Nora Vale

## 8. Confidence and Limitations

**Confidence Level: 92%**

**Supporting Factors:**
- Direct visual evidence (V04) shows Nora Vale carrying prototype out of east exit
- Access logs (D01_access.csv) confirm her presence in lab area during incident window
- No other staff member has opportunity or visual confirmation of being in lab area
- Timeline is consistent and corroborated by multiple sources

**Limitations:**
- All images are synthetic schematics, not photographs (per case brief)
- Coat color is evidence of appearance, not biometric identity
- Badge credentials log access but do not verify intent or authorization
- Service corridor is a camera blind spot (V10) - cannot rule out unauthorized entry via this route without additional evidence
- Witness statements are unverified and may be biased
- No information about Nora Vale's authorization status for taking the prototype

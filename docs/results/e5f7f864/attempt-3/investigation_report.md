## 7. Final Conclusion

**No responsible individual can be identified based on the available evidence.**

While Eli Stone had motive (expressed need for tomorrow's project) and opportunity (SERVICE_CORRIDOR entry at 18:14, a blind spot), the access log records badge credential use, not biometric identity. The two entries at SERVICE_CORRIDOR at 18:14 could belong to any combination of the five staff members. Maya Reed also entered via SERVICE_CORRIDOR at 18:14, but there is no evidence linking her to the theft. The 18:00-18:10 interval has no access log entries, meaning the prototype could have been removed before any of the recorded entries. Without visual evidence (V01-V10 unavailable in text-only track) or exit records, attribution is speculative.

## 8. Confidence and Limitations

**Confidence**: 0% (cannot identify responsible individual)

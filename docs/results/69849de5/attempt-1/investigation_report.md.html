Investigation Report: Laboratory Prototype Theft

1. Executive Summary

On 18:18, a laboratory prototype was discovered missing from the LAB. Based on visual evidence and access logs, Nora Vale (identified by her teal work coat) is the primary suspect. She was observed at the EAST EXIT at 18:14 in possession of the prototype.

2. Evidence Inventory

  • D01 (Access Logs): Records Nora Vale entering the East Corridor at 18:10 and exiting the East Exit at 18:14.
  • V02 (Lab Camera 18:00): Confirms prototype presence.
  • V04 (East Exit Camera 18:14): Shows a teal-coated individual with the prototype.
  • V05 (Lab Camera 18:18): Confirms prototype absence.
  • V06, V07, V08: Verify the locations of other staff (Theo Park, Ada Finch, Maya Reed) during the incident window.

3. Timeline

  • 18:00: Prototype confirmed in LAB (V02).
  • 18:10: Nora Vale enters EAST CORRIDOR (D01).
  • 18:11: Maya Reed at LOADING BAY (V08).
  • 18:14: Nora Vale exits EAST EXIT (D01); Prototype seen at EAST EXIT with a teal-coated person (V04).
  • 18:18: Prototype confirmed missing from LAB (V05).

4. Suspect Analysis

  • Nora Vale: Primary Suspect. Her movement matches the timeline of the prototype's disappearance. Most critically, visual evidence (V04) places the prototype with a person in her identified coat color at the exit at the exact time of her recorded exit.
  • Eli Stone: No direct evidence of presence in the lab during the window, though motive was established (D03).
  • Maya Reed: Observed at the loading bay; no evidence linking her to the lab or prototype.
  • Theo Park / Ada Finch: Verified to be in the cafeteria and reception respectively, away from the lab.

5. Key Deductions

  • The prototype was moved from its station in the LAB to the EAST EXIT between 18:00 and 18:14.
  • The teal-coated individual (Nora Vale) is the only person seen in the vicinity of the prototype's movement path (East Corridor/Exit) during this window.

6. Contradictions

  • Nora Vale claims she "only visited the east corridor and left," which is technically true according to logs, but fails to account for the prototype being seen with her at the exit.

7. Final Conclusion

Nora Vale is responsible for the removal of the prototype from the laboratory. The visual evidence at the East Exit (V04) is the smoking gun.

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: High (95%). The visual match of the coat color and the timing of the access logs are highly consistent.
  • Limitations: The service corridor is a blind spot, meaning other movements could have occurred unobserved, though no other suspects were placed in the area.

9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps

  • Review all footage from the loading bay area to see if the prototype was transferred to another vehicle or person.
  • Inspect Nora Vale's personal belongings and locker.

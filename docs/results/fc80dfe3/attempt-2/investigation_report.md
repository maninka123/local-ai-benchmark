# Investigation Report: Missing Laboratory Prototype
## Case 02 / V01-V10

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A laboratory prototype (black triangular object in gray container) was confirmed present at 18:00 and discovered missing at 18:18. Through analysis of access logs, witness statements, camera footage, and floor plans, **Eli Stone** is identified as the responsible individual.

**Key Finding:** The service door camera has a +5 minute clock offset (D03, V10). Eli Stone's appearance with the prototype at "18:19" on the service camera actually occurred at 18:14—within the theft window and through an unmonitored link to the lab.

## 5. Key Deductions

1. **Theft Window:** Prototype removed between 18:00 (V02) and 18:18 (V05)

2. **Clock Offset Critical:** Service camera shows +5 minute offset (D03, V10). Eli's "18:19" appearance is actually 18:14—within the theft window.

3. **Unmonitored Access:** Service Corridor provides unmonitored link to lab (V01), allowing theft without triggering main cameras.

4. **Badge vs Identity:** D03 notes doors log badge credentials, not biometric identity. However, coat colors match assigned identities consistently across all visual evidence.

5. **Single Point of Failure:** Only Eli Stone has both opportunity (service door access during theft window) and direct visual evidence of possession (V04).

## 7. Final Conclusion

**Eli Stone is responsible for the missing prototype.**

### Supporting Evidence:
1. **V04:** Visual confirmation of orange coat (Eli's assigned color) in possession of prototype at Service Door
2. **D03/V10:** Clock offset correction places Eli at 18:14, within theft window
3. **V01:** Service Corridor provides unmonitored access to lab
4. **D03:** Prior motive expressed ("needs that prototype tomorrow")
5. **D02:** Statement contradicted by visual evidence

### Alternative Explanations:
- Badge impersonation: Possible but unsupported; coat color matches Eli's assignment
- Prototype returned after 18:14: No evidence of return; V05 shows empty at 18:18

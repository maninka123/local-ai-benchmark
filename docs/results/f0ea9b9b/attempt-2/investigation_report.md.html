CASE_03: Missing Laboratory Prototype - Investigation Report

1. Executive Summary

This investigation examines the disappearance of a laboratory prototype between 18:00 and 18:18 on the date of incident. The prototype was last confirmed visible in the Lab at 18:00 (V02) and discovered missing at 18:18 (V05).

Key Findings:

  • Two individuals entered through the Service Door at 18:14 per access logs (D01_access.csv)
  • The Service Corridor is a camera blind spot (V10, D03_messages.md)
  • Coat colors cannot be reliably used for identification (D03_messages.md)
  • Eli Stone has established motive but his alibi contradicts the access log
  • Maya Reed's movements are consistent across all evidence

Conclusion: The prototype was removed via the Service Door corridor between 18:14 and 18:18. However, definitive identification of the responsible individual(s) is not possible with current evidence due to the blind spot and unreliable coat color descriptions.

7. Final Conclusion

The prototype was removed between 18:14 and 18:18 via the Service Door corridor. Two individuals entered through the Service Door at 18:14 per access log (Eli Stone and Maya Reed). However, definitive identification of who actually entered the Lab is not possible due to:

  1. Camera blind spot - Service corridor has no camera coverage (V10, D03_messages.md)
  2. Unreliable coat colors - Coat descriptions are from shift assignments, not actual appearance (D03_messages.md)
  3. No visual confirmation of Lab entry - V04 shows figures at Service Door but does not show them entering the Lab

Eli Stone has established motive (requested prototype at 17:00), but his witness statement contradicts the access log. Maya Reed's movements are consistent across all evidence, but her presence at the Service Door does not prove she entered the Lab.

The investigation cannot definitively identify a single responsible individual with confidence.

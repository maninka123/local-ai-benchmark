This investigation examines the disappearance of a laboratory prototype between 18:00 and 18:18 on the date of incident. The prototype was last confirmed visible in the Lab at 18:00 (V02) and discovered missing at 18:18 (V05).

## 7. Final Conclusion

**What most likely happened:**
The prototype was removed between 18:14 and 18:18 via the Service Door corridor. Two individuals logged as entering through the Service Door at 18:14 were Eli Stone and Maya Reed per the access log (D01_access.csv). However, definitive identification of who actually entered the Lab is not possible due to:

1. The Service corridor being a camera blind spot (V10, D03_messages.md)
2. Coat colors in schematics not matching actual appearance per D03_messages.md

**Observations supporting the conclusion:**
- V02 confirms prototype present at 18:00
- V04 shows two figures at Service Door at 18:14
- V05 shows prototype missing at 18:18
- D01_access.csv logs Eli Stone and Maya Reed entering Service Door at 18:14
- D03_messages.md explicitly states coat colors are unreliable for identification

**Alternative explanations that remain plausible:**
1. One or both individuals who entered via Service Door removed the prototype (HIGH plausibility)
2. Eli Stone acted alone and may have used Maya Reed as cover or she was unaware (MEDIUM plausibility)
3. Maya Reed acted alone and entered with someone else not logged in access system (LOW plausibility - access log would need to be compromised)

**Whether a responsible individual can be identified:**
NO. The evidence does not support definitive identification of a single responsible individual. While Eli Stone has motive and his alibi is contradicted, the blind spot and unreliable coat colors prevent visual confirmation of who actually entered the Lab.

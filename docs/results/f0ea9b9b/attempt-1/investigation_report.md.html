Alternative explanations that remain plausible:

Whether a responsible individual can be identified: NO. The evidence does not support definitive identification of a single responsible individual. While Eli Stone has motive and his alibi is contradicted, the blind spot and unreliable coat colors prevent visual confirmation of who actually entered the Lab.

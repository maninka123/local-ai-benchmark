## 1. Executive Summary

**Incident:** A laboratory prototype (gray box with black triangle marking) was removed from the Lab between 18:00 and 18:14 on the incident date.

**Key Finding:** Visual evidence shows a person wearing a teal work coat carrying the prototype at East Exit at 18:14 (V04). Badge records show Nora Vale's credential was used to enter East Corridor at 18:10 and exit East Exit at 18:14 (D01). Nora Vale is assigned a teal work coat per shift assignments (D03).

**Conclusion:** The evidence points to **Nora Vale** as the most likely responsible party. However, definitive identification is limited by:
- Badge credentials do not constitute biometric identification (D03)
- Coat colors are assigned per shift, not unique identifiers (D03)
- The theft moment was not captured on camera
- Service Corridor is an unmonitored blind spot (V10, D03)

**Confidence Level:** Moderate-High (0.75)

## 7. Final Conclusion

### What Most Likely Happened

Based on the available evidence, the most likely sequence of events is:

1. **Before 18:00:** Prototype was present in Lab (V02)
2. **18:00-18:10:** A person wearing a teal coat accessed the Lab (possibly via Service Corridor blind spot per V01, V10) and removed the prototype
3. **18:10:** The teal-coated person entered East Corridor (V03); Nora Vale's badge was used at this time (D01)
4. **18:14:** The teal-coated person exited via East Exit WITH THE PROTOTYPE (V04); Nora Vale's badge was used at this time (D01)
5. **18:18:** Prototype discovered missing from Lab (V05)

### Supporting Observations

- V04 provides the strongest direct evidence: teal-coated person with prototype at exit
- D01 badge records align temporally with visual observations of teal coat
- D03 confirms Nora Vale is assigned teal coat per shift
- D02 Nora Vale's statement contradicted by visual evidence

### Alternative Explanations (Plausible)

1. **Badge Impersonation:** Another person used Nora Vale's badge while wearing a teal coat (requires coordinated deception; possible but less likely)
2. **Authorized Removal:** Prototype legitimately removed with authorization not in available evidence (possible but no supporting documentation found)
3. **Coat Substitution:** Another person wore a teal coat assigned to someone else (possible given D03 shift assignment note)

### Can a Responsible Individual Be Identified?

**Yes, with calibrated confidence.** Nora Vale is the most likely responsible party based on:
- Convergence of visual evidence (teal coat with prototype)
- Badge record timing alignment
- Statement contradiction

However, **definitive identification cannot be established** due to limitations in badge credentials and coat color as identification methods.

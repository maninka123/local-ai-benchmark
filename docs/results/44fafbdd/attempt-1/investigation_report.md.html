5. Key Deductions

  1. Theft Method: Nora Vale accessed the laboratory through the Service Corridor (V01, V10), which is an unmonitored camera blind spot (D03). This allowed her to enter the lab without being recorded.

  2. Theft Timing: The theft occurred between 18:00 and 18:10, when Nora accessed the lab via the Service Corridor before appearing in the East Corridor at 18:10.

  3. Escape Route: After stealing the prototype, Nora exited through the monitored East Corridor → East Exit path, which is why she was captured on camera with the stolen item (V04).

  4. Statement Deception: Nora's witness statement claiming she "only visited the east corridor and left" is demonstrably false based on V04 showing her carrying the prototype.

  5. Other Suspects Cleared: Maya, Theo, and Ada all have visual alibis placing them elsewhere during the theft window. Eli has motive but no evidence of opportunity or access.

7. Final Conclusion

Nora Vale is responsible for the theft of the laboratory prototype.

Supporting Observations:

  1. Direct visual evidence: V04 shows a person in teal coat (Nora's assigned color per case brief) carrying the prototype out of East Exit at 18:14.
  2. Access log confirmation: D01 confirms Nora Vale entered East Corridor at 18:10 and exited East Exit at 18:14 - matching visual evidence exactly.
  3. False statement: Her claim in D02 that she "only visited the east corridor and left" is contradicted by V04 showing her with the stolen property.
  4. Opportunity via blind spot: The Service Corridor (V01, V10) provides unmonitored access to the lab, explaining how she stole the prototype without being seen entering.
  5. Other suspects cleared: All other staff have visual alibis during the theft window.

Alternative Explanations:

  • Badge impersonation: D03 notes doors log badge credentials, not biometric identity. However, the combination of coat color (teal = Nora), timing (matching her access logs), and possession of stolen property makes this implausible.
  • Coordinated theft: No evidence suggests multiple perpetrators; single actor explanation fits all evidence.

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Access logs corroborate visual evidence timing

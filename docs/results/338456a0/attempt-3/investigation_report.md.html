Investigation Report: Case_02 - Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

An investigation was conducted into the disappearance of a laboratory prototype, last seen at 18:00 and confirmed missing at 18:18. Analysis of security footage, badge access logs, and witness statements indicates that Eli Stone is the primary suspect. Evidence shows Stone entered the Service Door at 18:14 (corrected for a 5-minute clock offset) while carrying a box. This door provides access to the Service Corridor, which contains an unmonitored link to the Lab. Stone's statement that he did not approach the Lab is directly contradicted by his badge entry and the corresponding camera footage.

  • Theo Park (Blue): Entered Cafeteria at 18:12. No evidence of proximity to the Lab. Statement consistent with logs.
  • Ada Finch (Red): At Reception at 18:13. No evidence of proximity to the Lab. Statement consistent with logs.
  • Maya Reed (Purple): Seen moving a box at 18:11 (Loading Bay) and 18:12 (Cafeteria). Her movements are consistent with her statement and the logistics of moving consumables.
  • Eli Stone (Orange): Entered Service Door at 18:14 (corrected). Seen with a box. The Service Door leads to the Service Corridor, which has an unmonitored link to the Lab. His statement "I did not approach the Lab" is invalidated by his presence at the entry point to the unmonitored link.

5. Key Deductions

  • Access Path: The Service Door is the only documented entry point to the Service Corridor, which contains the unmonitored link to the Lab (V01).
  • Time Correlation: The 18:19 timestamp on V04, when corrected by the 5-minute offset (V10), perfectly matches Eli Stone's badge entry at 18:14 (D01).
  • Contradiction: Eli Stone's claim of non-proximity to the Lab is invalidated by his presence at the Service Door at 18:14, just four minutes before the prototype was confirmed missing.

6. Contradictions

  • Eli Stone's Statement: Claims he did not approach the Lab after 18:00, but badge logs and corrected camera footage place him at the Service Door (the gateway to the Lab's unmonitored link) at 18:14.

7. Final Conclusion

Eli Stone is the most likely individual responsible for the removal of the prototype. He utilized the Service Door at 18:14 to access the unmonitored link to the Lab, likely placing the prototype in the box he was observed carrying. His statement is a deliberate falsehood intended to distance himself from the crime scene.\n

8. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: 85%.
  • Limitations: Badge use is not biometric; coat color is not a definitive identifier; the unmonitored link to the Lab means the exact moment of removal cannot be visually confirmed. Identity is inferred from coat color and badge logs, which are not foolproof.

9. Recommended next investigative steps

  • Retrieve the cardboard box observed with Eli Stone.
  • Review footage from the Service Corridor (if any exists) or check for physical evidence of entry into the Lab via the unmonitored link.
  • Interview Eli Stone regarding the specific contents of the box he was carrying at 18:14.

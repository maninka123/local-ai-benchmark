# Investigation Report: Case_01 - Missing Laboratory Prototype

## 1. Executive Summary
The investigation into the theft of the laboratory prototype (last seen at 18:00, missing at 18:18) has identified **Nora Vale** as the primary suspect. Evidence shows Nora Vale entering the East Corridor with a cardboard box at 18:10 and exiting the facility at 18:14 with the prototype concealed within that box.

- **18:10**: Nora Vale enters East Corridor with a box (V03, D01).

## 4. Suspect Analysis
- **Nora Vale**:
    - **Opportunity**: Entered East Corridor (connected to Lab) at 18:10.
    - **Access**: Badge logs confirm entry/exit.
    - **Timeline**: Present in the corridor and exit during the theft window.
    - **Contradictions**: Claims she "only visited the east corridor and left," but V04 shows her leaving with the prototype.
    - **Evidence**: V03, V04, D01.
- **Maya Reed**:
    - **Opportunity**: At Loading Bay at 18:11.
    - **Access**: Moving a box as per D03.
    - **Timeline**: Consistent with her task.
    - **Evidence**: V08, D03.
- **Theo Park**:
    - **Opportunity**: In Cafeteria from 18:12.
    - **Evidence**: V06, D01.
- **Ada Finch**:
    - **Opportunity**: At Reception at 18:13.
    - **Evidence**: V07, D01.
- **Eli Stone**:
    - **Opportunity**: No recorded movement or visual evidence during the theft window.
    - **Evidence**: D02, D03.

## 5. Key Deductions
- The prototype was stolen between 18:00 and 18:18.
- Nora Vale was the only person seen moving toward the exit with a box containing the prototype (V04).
- Maya Reed's actions were consistent with a legitimate request (D03) to move a box to the loading bay.
- The Service Corridor (V10) is a blind spot, but Nora's path through the East Corridor and Exit was fully captured.

## 6. Contradictions
- Nora Vale's statement (D02) that she "only visited the east corridor and left" is contradicted by V04, which shows her leaving with the prototype.

## 7. Final Conclusion
**Nora Vale** is responsible for the theft. She used a cardboard box to transport the prototype from the Lab (via the East Corridor) to the East Exit.

## 8. Confidence and Limitations
- **Confidence**: 95%
- **Limitations**: While Nora's exit with the prototype is clear, the exact moment she entered the Lab is not captured by a camera (though the East Corridor is adjacent).

## 9. Recommended Next Investigative Steps
- Review badge logs for any unauthorized access to the Lab itself.
- Interview Nora Vale regarding the contents of the box she was carrying.
- Check the Loading Bay for any discarded materials from Maya Reed's box.

7. Final Conclusion

What Most Likely Happened:

Eli Stone, who had expressed a need for the prototype at 17:00 (D03), used his badge to access the Service Door at 18:14. The service corridor provides unmonitored direct access to the lab (V01, V10). During the window between 18:14 and 18:18, Eli Stone entered the lab via the service corridor and removed the prototype. He may have been accompanied by an accomplice (V04 shows two persons), or Maya Reed's badge was also used/misused during this incident.

Supporting Observations:

  • Eli's stated need for prototype (D03) - motive
  • Eli's badge used at Service Door at 18:14 (D01) - opportunity
  • Eli's false statement denying lab approach (D02) - deception

  • Prototype missing by 18:18 (V05) - result

Alternative Explanations:

## 5. Key Deductions

1. **Theft Confirmed**: Prototype was present at 18:00 (V02) and missing by 18:18 (V05). Removal occurred during this 18-minute window.

2. **Service Door Accessed**: Two badge events at Service Door at 18:14 (D01) coincide with visual evidence of two persons in gray coats (V04).

3. **Identity Uncertainty**: The gray coats visible at V04 do not match any assigned coat colors (teal, orange, purple, blue, red). This creates significant uncertainty about who actually used the badges.

4. **Blind Spot Exploitation**: Service corridor is confirmed as unmonitored (V01, V10), providing a route that cannot be visually tracked.

5. **Badge Limitations**: Security notes explicitly state "doors log badge credentials, not biometric identity" (D03). Badge use alone cannot confirm who physically entered.

6. **Coat Color Limitations**: Security notes state "coat descriptions are from shift assignments" (D03), meaning coat colors are not reliable identifiers.

## 7. Final Conclusion

**What Most Likely Happened**:

The prototype was removed from the lab between 18:00 and 18:18. Badge credentials for Eli Stone and Maya Reed were used at the Service Door at 18:14, but visual evidence shows two persons in gray coats that do not match their assigned coat colors. The service corridor blind spot prevented observation of any movements through this area.

**Supporting Observations**:

- V02 confirms prototype present at 18:00
- V05 confirms prototype missing by 18:18
- D01 shows Eli Stone and Maya Reed badges used at Service Door 18:14
- V04 shows two gray-coated persons at Service Door 18:14 - no color match to assignments
- V01 and V10 confirm service corridor is unmonitored blind spot
- D03 states badges log credentials not biometric identity; coat colors are shift assignments

**Alternative Explanations**:

1. **Badge Lending/Theft**: Someone else used Eli Stone's and/or Maya Reed's badge(s) to access the service door
2. **Coat Change/Disguise**: Badge holders changed into gray coats before entering service door (no evidence observed)
3. **Removal via East Corridor**: Prototype removed through monitored east corridor route instead of service corridor (no visual evidence)
4. **Multiple Perpetrators**: Two or more individuals coordinated the theft

**Can a Responsible Individual Be Identified?**

**NO**. The evidence does not support definitive identification of a responsible individual.

**Reasons**:
1. Badge use does not confirm biometric identity (D03)
2. Coat colors are shift assignments, not identifiers (D03)
3. Visual evidence at Service Door shows gray coats - no match to any staff member's assigned color
4. No visual evidence shows who entered the lab or removed the prototype
5. Multiple unobserved intervals during incident window
6. Witness statements are unverified

**Confidence Level**: 25%

This low confidence reflects that while we know a theft occurred and which badges were used, we cannot determine who actually committed the act.

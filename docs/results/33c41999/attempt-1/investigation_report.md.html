Investigation Report: Missing Laboratory Prototype

1. Executive Summary

A prototype was last confirmed present in the laboratory at 18:00 and discovered missing at 18:18. Analysis of access logs, camera footage, witness statements, and internal communications identifies Eli Stone as the most likely person responsible for removing the prototype. Eli entered via the SERVICE_DOOR at 18:14, which connects to the laboratory through an unmonitored service corridor. He was visually confirmed with the prototype at the service door at actual time 18:14 (camera timestamp 18:19 corrected for a +5 minute offset). His statement that he did not approach the lab after 18:00 is contradicted by direct evidence.

  • Statement: "I only visited the east corridor and left." Consistent with V03 and D01.
  • Assessment: Low suspicion. No evidence linking her to the prototype or the service area.

  • Statement: "I carried a cardboard supply box at 18:11." Consistent with V08 and D03.
  • Assessment: Low suspicion. Her movements are accounted for and unrelated to the LAB or SERVICE_DOOR.

  • Statement: "I remained in the cafeteria from 18:12." Consistent with V06 and D01.

Eli Stone (Orange Coat)

  • Opportunity: Entered SERVICE_DOOR at 18:14 (D01). This door connects to the unmonitored SERVICE_CORRIDOR which links to the LAB (V01).
  • Motive: Stated need for prototype for his project at 17:00 (D03).
  • Evidence: Visually confirmed with prototype at SERVICE_DOOR at actual time 18:14 (V04, V10). Access log confirms entry at 18:14 (D01).
  • Statement: "I did not approach the lab after 18:00." Contradicted by V04 and D01.
  • Assessment: High suspicion. Direct visual evidence places him with the prototype during the critical window. His statement is false.

4. Key Deductions

  1. Access Path: The SERVICE_DOOR (V01) provides access to the LAB via an unmonitored corridor, bypassing camera coverage.
  2. Timing: Eli's entry at 18:14 (D01) and presence with prototype at 18:14 (V04 corrected) aligns perfectly with the disappearance window (18:00-18:18).
  3. Camera Offset: The service camera's +5 minute offset (V10, D03) is critical. Without correction, Eli would appear to be at 18:19, potentially after the prototype was already gone. Correction places him during the event.
  4. Exculpatory Evidence for Others: All other suspects have consistent statements and visual/log evidence placing them in unrelated locations during the critical window.

5. Contradictions

  • Eli Stone's Statement vs. Evidence: Eli claimed he did not approach the lab after 18:00 (D02). However, D01 shows him entering SERVICE_DOOR at 18:14, and V04 shows him with the prototype at that time. This is a direct contradiction.
  • No Contradictions for Others: Nora, Maya, Theo, and Ada's statements are consistent with their respective logs and camera footage.

6. Final Conclusion

Eli Stone most likely removed the prototype from the laboratory between 18:14 and 18:18 via the SERVICE_DOOR. He had motive (project need), opportunity (access to SERVICE_DOOR), and was visually confirmed with the prototype at the service entrance during the critical window. His statement denying approach to the lab is contradicted by evidence.

7. Confidence and Limitations

  • Confidence: High. Direct visual evidence (V04) places Eli with the prototype. Access log (D01) confirms his entry. Motive (D03) is established. Other suspects are exonerated by consistent alibis.
  • Limitations: Cannot definitively prove intent (theft vs. unauthorized removal for work). Cannot confirm if he successfully exited with the prototype or was intercepted. Service corridor access logs (if any) were not provided.

8. Recommended Next Steps

  1. Interview Eli Stone: Confront him with V04 and D01 evidence regarding his presence at SERVICE_DOOR with the prototype.
  2. Verify Prototype Usage: Check if the prototype was used in Eli's project or found in his possession.
  3. Service Corridor Logs: Investigate if any access logs exist for the unmonitored service corridor.
  4. Review Security Protocols: Assess why the SERVICE_DOOR allows access to an unmonitored area and consider adding surveillance or access controls.

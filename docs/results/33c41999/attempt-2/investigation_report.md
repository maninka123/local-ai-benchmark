# CASE_02: Missing Laboratory Prototype - Investigation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

A prototype (grey box with black triangle symbol) was confirmed present in the LAB at 18:00 and discovered missing at 18:18. Five staff members were active in the facility during this window, each observed in different locations wearing distinct colored work coats. One individual (orange coat, badge record for Eli Stone) was visually documented with the object at the SERVICE DOOR at 18:14 (corrected from displayed 18:19). However, due to identity limitations inherent in the evidence (badge credentials ≠ biometric identity; coat colors ≠ personal identification), no definitive attribution of responsibility can be established. The most probable sequence involves unauthorized access through the unmonitored service corridor link, but alternative explanations remain plausible.

## 5. Key Deductions

1. **Object removal window**: Between 18:00 (confirmed present) and 18:18 (confirmed absent)
2. **Service corridor access**: The unmonitored link between SERVICE CORRIDOR and LAB (V01) provides a plausible route for object removal
3. **Eli Stone's presence with object**: V04 shows person in orange coat with object at SERVICE DOOR at 18:14; this is the only visual evidence of anyone with the prototype
4. **Identity limitations**: Badge records log credentials, not biometric identity (D03); coat colors identify appearance at specific moments only
5. **Camera offset correction**: V10 confirms service camera has +5 minute offset; 18:19 displayed = 18:14 actual time
6. **No LAB surveillance gap**: No visual evidence places anyone in the LAB between 18:00 and 18:18 except through unmonitored links

## 6. Contradictions

### Primary Contradiction
- **Eli Stone's statement vs. visual evidence**: Eli states "I did not approach the lab after 18:00" (unverified statement), but V04 shows a person in orange coat (identified as Eli by badge record D01) with the object at SERVICE DOOR at 18:14, which connects to LAB via unmonitored link

### Secondary Issues
- **Badge vs. identity**: Badge records show credential use, not necessarily the named individual wearing them (D03)
- **Coat color ambiguity**: Coat descriptions are from shift assignments (D03); someone could wear another's coat
- **Timeline gaps**: No evidence for 17:20-18:00 or 18:14-18:18 intervals

## 7. Final Conclusion

### Most Likely Scenario
The prototype was removed from the LAB between 18:00 and 18:18 via the unmonitored service corridor link (V01). The person in orange coat observed with the object at SERVICE DOOR at 18:14 (V04) is the strongest visual evidence of involvement. Badge record D01 associates this credential with Eli Stone, and internal message D02 shows motive (project need). However, due to identity limitations documented in D03, we cannot definitively attribute this to Eli Stone personally.

### Responsible Individual Assessment
**Cannot be definitively identified.** While Eli Stone's badge was used at SERVICE_DOOR and he was visually documented with the object, the evidence does not prove:
- That Eli wore the orange coat (vs. someone else using his credentials)
- That the person in V04 is biometrically Eli Stone
- That the object was removed from LAB (vs. moved to service area for legitimate reasons)

### Plausible Alternative Explanations
1. **Credential theft**: Someone used Eli's badge and wore orange coat to access service door; object removal by different person
2. **Legitimate transfer**: Object moved to service area for authorized purpose; removed from LAB before 18:00 or after 18:18 by different party
3. **Misidentification**: Person in V04 is not Eli Stone but someone wearing orange coat and using stolen credentials
4. **Timing error**: Object removed before 18:00 (V02 shows it present, but timing of confirmation uncertain)

## 8. Confidence and Limitations
